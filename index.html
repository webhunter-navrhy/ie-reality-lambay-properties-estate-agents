Accommodation

The accommodation opens with an own-door entrance offering maximum privacy and convenience, leading into a bright, light-filled living area featuring large floor-to-ceiling windows. The fully equipped kitchen features an electric hob, oven, extractor fan, washer/dryer, dishwasher, and full-size fridge-freezer.The rear hallway leads to two bedrooms: a large master bedroom with built-in floor-to-ceiling wardrobes, and a second small double bedroom also featuring built-in wardrobes and double patio doors opening directly onto a private, sunny south-facing rear garden with convenient pedestrian access. A modern family bathroom with a full-sized bath, overhead shower, and space for extra storage completes the layout.
